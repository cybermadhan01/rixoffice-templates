# <Post title>

## The hook

Open with the concrete thing: a number, a failure, or a question someone actually asked. No throat-clearing.

## Why this matters

Two or three paragraphs on the problem and who feels it. Give the reader a reason to keep reading before the first technical detail.

## What we tried

The approach, in enough detail that a reader could try it. Include what did not work.

## What happened

Results, with numbers and a timeframe. Include the disappointing parts.

## What we would do differently

The honest retrospective. This section is the reason people read technical posts twice.

## Try it yourself

Steps, links, and any prerequisites. A reader should be able to reproduce this without asking you anything.
